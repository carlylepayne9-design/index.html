01 · BUSINESS & PRODUCT PREPARATION

Build your product catalog

  • Source secondhand, vintage, wholesale and dead-stock items.
  • Choose clear categories and focused niches.
  • Photograph every item from multiple angles with detail shots.
  • Write clear names and descriptions covering material, size, features, condition and included items.
  • Set regular and sale prices and document discounts or subscription pricing.
  • Publish shipping methods, timelines, costs and free-shipping thresholds.
  • Publish return acceptance rules, time limits and the refund process.
02 · BRANDING & LEGAL

Make the business recognizable

  • Use the 4STARAPPEALS logo and define your brand colors.
  • Choose and secure your business domain.
  • Confirm business registration, resale permits and seller-license requirements for your location.
03 · WEBSITE & PLATFORM

Build the shopping experience

  • Choose an ecommerce platform such as Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce or another provider.
  • Use a mobile-friendly theme with simple navigation and clear Add to Cart buttons.
  • Connect secure payment options such as cards and supported digital wallets.
  • Offer accounts for order history, wishlists and loyalty features if desired.
04 · TRUST & INFORMATION

Answer customer questions

  • Create an About page with your story, mission and contact information.
  • Add an FAQ covering shipping, returns and product care.
  • Collect customer reviews and other authentic social proof.
05 · MARKETING & SEO

Help shoppers find you

  • Use relevant keywords in product titles, descriptions and meta tags.
  • Add social media links and product-sharing options.
  • Offer email signup with early access or a launch discount.
PAYMENTS

Connect a real payment provider

The checkout now supports Card, PayPal and digital-wallet choices in the front end. For real transactions, connect a provider such as Stripe or PayPal on the server so card data is never stored by this website.

TAX SETTINGS

Sales tax

Tax is calculated at checkout based on the selected shipping state. Rates below are demo rates and should be replaced with your actual tax provider/rules before taking live payments.

06 · TESTING & LAUNCH

Check everything before launch

  • Test checkout, payments and shipping on desktop and mobile.
  • Check page speed, image sizes and responsive layouts.
  • Publish when ready, then monitor sales, customer feedback and inventory.
  • Keep listings, pricing and stock counts updated regularly.

OUR STORY

About 4STARAPPEALS

Four people. One vision. One journey.

4STARAPPEALS was started by Aaron, Xavier, Jared, and Ashton with one simple goal: to find a way to make money and build something of our own.

We tried different side hustles. Some worked for a little while. Others didn’t work at all. But every failure taught us something and pushed us to keep looking for the right opportunity.

Then we found reselling.

What started as another attempt to make money quickly became something much bigger. We started learning how to find products, understand the market, build a brand, serve customers, and create our own opportunities. Eventually, we took it a step further by building our own website and creating a real business around what we were doing.
